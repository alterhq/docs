## Speaker identification

Meeting transcripts go through three layers: **diarization** (who spoke when), **speaker identity** (saved voices on your Mac), and optional **cloud name matching** (calendar and Contacts hints).

### Speaker diarization

Alter uses **Pyannote 4 (Community-1)** for speaker diarization — state-of-the-art AI that separates speakers and labels them as **Speaker 1**, **Speaker 2**, and so on. Diarization runs locally on your Mac.

When the diarization engine is **Nemotron 3**, Alter still derives voiceprints from Pyannote so saved voices stay compatible across engines.

### Speaker identity (saved voices)

Speaker identity remembers voices you have named before and applies those names automatically in future meetings — without sending voice data to the cloud.

<Info>
  **100% local.** Saved voiceprints live in `~/Library/Application Support/Alter/SpeakerIdentity/`. They are biometric data and stay on your Mac like the rest of Application Support.
</Info>

#### How matching works

After diarization, Alter compares each speaker's voiceprint against your **saved voices**. When a match is confident, generic labels like **Speaker 1** are replaced with the saved name before the transcript is shown.

If two saved voices could both match the same speaker, Alter opens a **review sheet** instead of guessing. You can listen to a clip of that speaker, pick the right name for this meeting, and optionally update saved voices for the future.

#### How voices get saved

Alter builds saved voices from meeting audio — you never configure them in a separate settings screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rename a speaker in the transcript">
    Open a meeting transcript, click a speaker chip, and choose a real name from the picker (or type one). Names with a **waveform** icon already have a saved voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose scope when prompted">
    **This meeting only** renames the transcript. **Future meetings** also saves or updates the voiceprint so Alter recognizes that person next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review ambiguous matches">
    When Alter is unsure, a review sheet appears after transcription. Listen to the clip, confirm the name, and choose whether to keep, merge, or forget related saved voices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Saved voices can also be created automatically when **Find speakers names (experimental)** resolves a generic label to a real name from calendar or Contacts data.

#### Managing saved voices in a transcript

| Action | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Rename speaker | Speaker chip → name picker | Rename for this meeting and optionally save the voice for future meetings |
| Review conflict | Sheet after transcription | Resolve ambiguous matches between saved voices |
| Remove voice data | Right-click speaker chip → **Remove voice data** | Removes voice data for that speaker in **this meeting only** (does not delete a saved voice globally) |
| Merge or forget | Conflict review sheet → **Future voice recognition** | Combine duplicate saved voices or stop recognizing selected names in future meetings |

<Tip>
  Updating saved voices affects **future** recognition only. Past transcripts keep the names they had when you saved them.
</Tip>

#### Limitations

- Requires diarization with usable voice data — if a meeting has no voiceprint for a speaker, you can still rename the transcript but cannot save that voice for the future.
- Each saved identity keeps up to **five** voice samples; very distinct speaking styles may not fit in one identity.
- Alter applies a saved name only when the match is confident. Close calls are sent to review instead of auto-renamed.
- There is no global list of saved voices in Settings yet — manage them from meeting transcripts and conflict review.

### Cloud speaker name matching (beta93+)

When cloud transcription is available, enable **Find speakers names (experimental)** in **Settings → Voice → Automation**. This optional layer suggests real names from your calendar and Contacts when generic labels remain after local voice matching:

- **Calendars for speaker matching** — Choose which Apple calendars to use. Alter matches the recording to calendar events and attendee lists.
- **Contacts for speaker matching** — Grant Contacts access so attendee emails resolve to display names.
- **Manual calendar event selection** — Link a transcript to a specific calendar event when auto-match is wrong.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Voice settings">
    **Settings → Voice → Automation**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable speaker matching">
    Turn on **Find speakers names (experimental)**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select calendars">
    Pick the calendars that hold your work meetings (or select all)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Contacts">
    Allow Contacts when prompted for attendee name resolution
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cloud matching requires login and internet access. Local diarization and speaker identity still run on your Mac first.

Cloud processing requires login and fair-use eligibility; local **Whisper Pro** and **Parakeet** options remain fully offline.

## File Storage

### Location

All recordings and transcripts are stored locally at:

```
~/Library/Application Support/Alter/Transcripts
```

<Info>
  **100% Private:** Everything stays on your Mac. No cloud storage unless you explicitly choose cloud processing.
</Info>

<Warning>
  If you uninstall Alter with a cleanup tool such as CleanMyMac, it can remove your local transcripts and related data. To switch versions, replace the app in `Applications` instead. See [Reinstall or downgrade Alter safely](/how-to/reinstall-or-downgrade-alter).
</Warning>
